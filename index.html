Amphitheater OS

The operating system for a live venue and talent buyer

A venue does not run on a calendar. It runs on the night — where a date cannot go on sale until the contract is signed and the deposit is in, where the advance and the riders decide whether the show loads in clean, and where the settlement a tour manager signs before the bus leaves is rebuilt by hand in most rooms after doors close. Amphitheater OS is built around that.

The one-click settlement sheet

Gross off the box office, less taxes, fees and every expense, the deal applied, the artist's cut — the sheet a tour manager signs, in one click.

Nothing goes on sale unbooked
